Cross-site probing simulator for the Public Hash List

Models the attack where a tracker embedded on many sites probes requestFileHandle() for n Public Hash List hashes on each site and links visits whose bit vectors agree. The math, the calibration against the real 438,684-entry list, and the findings are in probing-attack-model.md. Defaults reproduce the VERSION: 2026-09-14 snapshot's composition. Everything is an assumption you can change; nothing here is a measurement of real users.

Channel

Read is the probing attack of §§1–12. Write turns COS into a store: the tracker writes a chosen pattern on site A and reads it back on site B. See §13 of probing-attack-model.md.

Write channel

Aging: a written resource survives to the readback with probability ρaging, where ρ is its tier persistence. 0 reads back immediately; higher ages the cache between the write on site A and the read on site B. Storing-origin mode also needs the embedder to grant the cross-origin-storage Permissions Policy (default self).

Generative site graph (site mode only)

Communities stand in for co-deployment: the sites running the same CMS, theme, or plugin bundle. With clustering at 0, every resource picks its sites independently and two resources almost never share one; at 1, resources cluster tightly and their site sets intersect sharply. This is the parameter the intersection attack of §7 turns on, and it is not one the list itself controls.
